1ClickDL 1.14.0

- Desktop application with Google sign-in, a free allowance of 3 download authorizations per account per day, and an annual Pro licence.
- YouTube to editing-friendly MP4, browser extension, manual queue and interrupted-download resume.
- Automatic YouTube session routing: other platforms never receive YouTube cookies, including in mixed queues.
- A replayable interactive tutorial highlights the real controls. Chrome extension installation includes the exact Developer mode, Load unpacked and Pin steps.
- YouTube playlists require Pro; free accounts download individual videos only. TikTok video and Instagram Reel support remains experimental; Instagram carousel posts and X/Twitter are not included.
- Signed update feeds are separate from operating-system publisher signatures.

Initial installers are not Apple-notarized or Authenticode-signed. Follow the installation guide on https://1clickdl.com/fr/guide/ and download only official distribution files.

macOS 13+ on Apple Silicon. Windows 10/11 x64 on Intel or AMD. Internet access and a Google software account are required. Download only content you are entitled to use.

The separately bundled open-source tools retain their own licences and are unchanged from 1.13.0. Their available upstream notices and source materials remain accessible at https://github.com/faridafbprod-dev/1clickdl-releases/releases/tag/v1.13.0.
