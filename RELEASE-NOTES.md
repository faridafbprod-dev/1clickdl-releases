1ClickDL 1.13.0

- Desktop application with Google sign-in, a free allowance of 3 download authorizations per account per day, and an annual Pro licence.
- YouTube to editing-friendly MP4, browser extension, manual queue and interrupted-download resume.
- TikTok video and Instagram Reel support remains experimental; YouTube playlists and Instagram carousel posts are not part of this initial commercial release.
- Signed update feeds are separate from operating-system publisher signatures.

Initial installers are not Apple-notarized or Authenticode-signed. Follow the installation guide on https://1clickdl.com/fr/guide/ and download only official distribution files.

macOS 13+ on Apple Silicon. Windows 10/11 x64 on Intel or AMD. Internet access and a Google software account are required. Download only content you are entitled to use.
